Privacy Policy

Your listening stays on your phone

Last updated November 25, 2026 · applies to the iPhone and Android apps

The short version

The Growing Christians app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Which talks you've heard and the dates, where you stopped in each talk, your notes, and your rounds.
  • Talks you choose to download for offline listening.
  • Your settings (playback speed, the auto-remove downloads switch).

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. It is not synced to iCloud or Google — deleting the app deletes it. If you use Export Backup, the app makes a file and hands it to you; where you save or send that file is up to you.

What the app connects to

The app only connects to Growing Christians Ministries' website (growingchristians.org and media.growingchristians.org) to:

  • stream or download the talk audio you choose to play or download, and
  • occasionally look up a talk's audio link on its web page.

These are ordinary web requests, like visiting the site in a browser. The app sends nothing about you or your listening history. The ministry's website has its own policies.

The website, Study Notes and Donate links open in your web browser. Any donation is made on the ministry's own donation page, never inside the app.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions — no location, contacts, camera, or microphone. On Android it uses internet access and the standard permission to keep playing audio in the background.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions: azbarks@gmail.com.
