What it does

Made for listening day after day

▶️

Listen anywhere

Talks play with the screen off, with lock-screen controls, adjustable speed, and 15-second rewind.

✅

Know what you've heard

A talk is checked off when it plays to the end, with the date. Re-listens are counted too.

⏯️

Pick up where you left off

Continue and Next Unheard take you straight to the right talk. Stopped halfway? It resumes.

📊

See your progress

Progress for every book and testament, and how much you've listened this week, month and year.

🔁

Start a new round

Heard them all? Start Over unchecks every talk — and keeps your full history.

⬇️

Offline downloads

Download a talk or a whole book for the car or a trip. Downloads can clear themselves once heard.
